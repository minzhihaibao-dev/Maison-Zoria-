Chapter II

Savoir-Faire & Atelier Mastery

Uncompromising standards passed down through master leather artisans in Geneva, Paris, and Florence.

Traditional Saddle Stitching

Each seam is bound using double-needle linen thread saddle stitching—a technique untouched by modern industrial machinery. If a single loop breaks, the surrounding stitch remains intact for over a century.

  • Hand-burnished beeswax edging
  • Custom 18K solid gold electroplated buckles
  • French vegetable-tanned full grain calfskin

Micro-Precision Detailing

Every bag incorporates microscopic horological precision. Metal fittings are polished using diamond paste to achieve a mirror finish worthy of fine Swiss watchmaking.
